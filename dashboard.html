02 / WHAT CHANGED
D

Brand system

Replaced the six-loop gradient logo with a compact, single-color D mark and a simpler wordmark. Updated favicon and dark CTA mark.

↗

Buttons

Removed the gradient borders, halos, and oversized pill corners. Buttons now use a compact 12px radius, clear hierarchy, and quiet hover lift.

◌

Motion

Removed page-wide drifting shapes and height-changing feed rows. Product scenes use contained animation and an explicit pause control.

□

Product scenes

Replaced generic zap, layers, routing, and chart icons with custom scenes: response race, pipeline, routing, AI orbit, data hub, campaign, and reporting.

▣

Rep stories

Reframed the editorial section as larger device and dashboard compositions with phone, manager overview, and next-best-action cards.

@

Contact

Updated demo and footer contact points to support@drivecentric.space as requested.
